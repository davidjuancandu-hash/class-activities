Your pocket guide

Costa Rican mascaradas: giant masked characters dance with live cimarrona music. The national celebration is on October 31. Artists create expressive faces and costumes.

Halloween in California: many people wear costumes, visit pumpkin patches, and go trick-or-treating on October 31. A carved pumpkin with a light inside is a jack-o’-lantern.

Day of the Dead in Mexico: mainly November 1–2. Families remember loved ones who have died. Ofrendas can include photographs, favorite foods, candles, and marigolds. Decorated skulls can express remembrance.

Communities are diverse. People can enjoy more than one tradition. Similar decorations can have different meanings.
