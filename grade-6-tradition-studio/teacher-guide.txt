TRADITION STUDIO — GRADE 8
30-MINUTE CLASS PLAN
Same public URL; the grade-6 folder name is retained so existing links work.
All student-facing content is English. No video required.

0–2 minutes: enter identity, introduce three traditions and respectful comparisons.
2–7: Costa Rica field notes, six-word detail, five comprehension missions.
7–8: Lantern Blaster — 60 active seconds, tap/click or arrow keys + Space.
8–13: California field notes, six-word detail, five missions.
13–16: Photo Vault — six pairs of real photographs; read captions after matching.
16–21: Mexico field notes, six-word detail, five missions.
21–22: Mix-Up Machine final boss — 60 active seconds, changing tradition target.
22–28: Pumpkin Lab and Mask Workshop, approximately three minutes each.
28–30: compare traditions with a partner, explain designs, finish and download.
This is teacher-guided suggested pacing, not a guaranteed 30-minute lock. Reading speed, memory matching, and design time vary. Project a timer for workshops. Early finishers explain an additional similarity and difference or revise a design.

PROGRESSION
Each field-note page requires 60 active seconds plus a six-word note. Questions have an eight-second minimum on first reach, a selected response, and hints. Five missions unlock that level's game. Games must be completed before the next culture unlocks. Completed games may be replayed or continued. The full photo-memory board must be matched, with unlimited attempts. Arcade rounds have no minimum score, no elimination, and no human targets. Timers pause on hidden tabs and while the Pocket Guide is open. Refresh may restart an unfinished reading/game round; completed work is saved.

LEARNING
Mascarada: community performance, artisan skill, cimarrona music, La Giganta, national symbol in 2022.
California Halloween: costumes, trick-or-treating, pumpkins, entertainment, suspense, variation in participation.
Mexico Day of the Dead: November 1–2, remembrance, ofrendas, symbolic objects, regional and family variation.
Compare purposes and distinguish facts from opinions. Similar-looking decorations can have different meanings. Traditions are not exclusive to national borders. No student needs to discuss a deceased relative.

ASSESSMENT
15 comprehension questions, 1 point each. Correctness stays hidden until FINISH.
Answer key: 1 B, 2 A, 3 C, 4 B, 5 A, 6 C, 7 B, 8 A, 9 C, 10 C, 11 A, 12 B, 13 C, 14 B, 15 C.
Game points, memory attempts, artistic ability, and personal preferences do not change academic score.
Designs each require a name and TWO adjectives. Download designs as PNG plus the text quest record with identity, notes, comprehension results, and game scores. Submit in Classroom if directed.
Autosave is browser-local, same browser/device, no accounts or server collection. New Grade 8 save key keeps old Grade 6 work separate. Shared computers should use separate browser profiles.
