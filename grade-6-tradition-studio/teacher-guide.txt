TRADITION STUDIO — GRADE 6
Companion to Masks, Pumpkins and Memories PowerPoint.
Suggested time: about 20 minutes; 4 minutes guessing, 5 minutes per design, 4 minutes speaking, 2 minutes saving. Flexible creative activity, not a guaranteed minimum duration.

1. GUESS THE TRADITION
Nine clues, one at a time, three choices: Costa Rican Mascarada, Halloween in California, Day of the Dead in Mexico. New clues require 8 active seconds and a selected response. Previous/reached clues can be reviewed. Hints and the Pocket Guide provide help. Correctness stays hidden until final completion.
Answers in order: Costa Rica, California, Mexico, Costa Rica, California, Mexico, Costa Rica, California, Mexico. Each correct guess earns 1 point, total 9. Interpret clues in the context of the lesson; traditions can travel and communities are diverse.

2. PUMPKIN LAB
Choose pumpkin color, eyes, nose, mouth, and glow. Surprise Me changes the design. Give it a name and choose TWO adjectives. Save picture as PNG. Name and two adjectives unlock the next workshop. No physical tools.

3. MASCARADA MASK WORKSHOP
Create an invented character inspired by Costa Rican giant masks. Choose colors, facial features, and spots/stripes/stars. Name it and choose two adjectives. Download the picture. A creative interpretation, not a claim to reproduce an authentic artisan design.

4. GALLERY AND SPEAKING
Students describe both creations to a partner using the supplied sentence models. Choose a presentation preference/reason, then FINISH + REVEAL MY CLUE SCORE. They may edit designs or review guesses before finishing. Artistic ability and personal choices do not affect clue score. No forced sharing of memories about deceased relatives.

SUBMISSION
Each student can download 2 PNG pictures plus a text Studio Record showing identity, 9-point clue score, design names/adjectives, preference, and completion. Submit files through Classroom when directed. PNG includes student name and section. Save on the student's own device/account.

STORAGE
Browser-local autosave, no server uploads or accounts. Same-device refresh resumes work. Does not sync between devices. Use separate browser profiles on shared computers. No reset button in student view. Finalized clues lock after finishing. Browser storage or downloads may be restricted by school policy; check on classroom devices.
