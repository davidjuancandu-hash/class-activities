Travel Writing Quest — Pages 86–87

24 questions • hidden 12-second minimum per new question • no countdown • one question at a time • 6 themed levels

Objectives

Write and identify concluding sentences (restatement, opinion, prediction, suggestion); use because/since for reasons and so for results; identify punctuation and sentence relationships. Based only on the page 86–87 writing and grammar reference.

Answer Key

  1. Where is the concluding sentence usually found?
    Correct: At the end
    The last sentence is usually the concluding sentence.
  2. Which is a way to conclude a paragraph?
    Correct: Restate the main idea
    The page lists restating the main idea.
  3. How should a restatement express the main idea?
    Correct: Using different words
    Restate the main idea using different words.
  4. Which strategy recommends an action?
    Correct: Suggestion
    A suggestion recommends something.
  5. “In summary, travel has physical, emotional, and mental benefits.” What kind of ending is this?
    Correct: Restatement
    The example is a restatement.
  6. “I believe that experiential travel is better for travelers and locals.” What kind?
    Correct: Opinion
    “I believe” expresses an opinion.
  7. “You will understand Vietnam better if you take the Mekong River tour.” What kind?
    Correct: Prediction
    It predicts a future result.
  8. “If you want a relaxing vacation, I suggest you take a hot springs tour of Japan.” What kind?
    Correct: Suggestion
    “I suggest” gives a recommendation.
  9. Which phrase signals an opinion?
    Correct: I believe
    “I believe” signals the writer’s view.
  10. Which phrase signals a suggestion?
    Correct: I suggest
    “I suggest” recommends an action.
  11. Which phrase most clearly signals a prediction?
    Correct: You will
    “You will” points to a future result.
  12. Which phrase most clearly signals a restatement?
    Correct: In summary
    “In summary” signals a restatement.
  13. What is the topic sentence about?
    Correct: A volunteer work trip
    The provided topic sentence is about a volunteer work trip.
  14. According to the topic sentence, what can a volunteer trip provide?
    Correct: Job experience
    Job experience is explicitly listed.
  15. What can travelers learn on a volunteer work trip?
    Correct: Local culture
    The sentence says they learn about local culture.
  16. Which choice best RESTATES the volunteer-trip topic sentence?
    Correct: Improving work skills and helping others while you learn about a new place is a great way to travel.
    Choice C restates all three ideas using different words.
  17. Which word can introduce a reason?
    Correct: Because
    Because introduces a reason.
  18. Which word introduces a result?
    Correct: So
    So introduces a result.
  19. When a because/since clause comes first, what punctuation is used after it?
    Correct: Comma
    The grammar box says use a comma when the reason clause comes first.
  20. In that sentence, what is the reason?
    Correct: They stay with local people
    Staying with local people is the reason.
  21. In “Travelers stay with local people, so they learn more about a place,” what is the result?
    Correct: They learn more about a place
    The so clause gives the result.
  22. Choose the correct result connector: “Travelers stay with local people, ___ they learn more about a place.”
    Correct: so
    So introduces the result.
  23. Choose the correctly punctuated sentence.
    Correct: Since they stay with local people, travelers learn more.
    A comma follows the initial since clause.
  24. What is the relationship in “Since they stay with local people, experiential travelers learn more about a place”?
    Correct: Reason followed by result
    Since introduces the reason first.
