Separate the imagined opening scene from the narrator’s real location after waking. Notice the comparison he uses for the brain. Listen for what happens to the body and why chemicals and hormones are mentioned.

👁️ Sleep stages · 1:14–1:54

Listen for a repeating pattern, an approximate number range, and a three-letter abbreviation. Match the letters to the words used in the video. Picture the movement shown in the eye animation.

🧩 Dream explanations · 2:09–3:14

Does the narrator sound certain? Separate a proposed explanation from proof. Compare cautious words such as may or could with absolute words such as always or definitely. The joke about researchers is not evidence.

⏳ Numbers and beliefs · 3:33–3:58

Keep yearly and lifetime estimates separate. Identify the units: days or years. Distinguish what ancient people believed from what science has demonstrated.
