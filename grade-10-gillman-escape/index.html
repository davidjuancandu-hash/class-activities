BLACK LAGOON STUDIOSGRADE 8 · AFTER THE PRESENTATION

The Creature Case File

Universal released Creature from the Black Lagoon in 1954. Its fictional expedition visits the Amazon. The creature combines a human-like body with aquatic features such as gills, scales, and webbed fingers.

Milicent Patrick developed the design. Jack Kevan made the bodysuit. Chris Mueller Jr. sculpted the head mask. Bud Westmore supervised Universal’s makeup department and contributed to the work.

Ben Chapman played the creature on land. Ricou Browning performed underwater. The costume was a physical effect, not a computer-generated character.

Patrick received no on-screen credit for her design contribution. Mallory O’Meara’s 2019 book, The Lady from the Black Lagoon, brought renewed attention to her career. Fair credits identify people and their contributions.
