Your quick guide

Costa Rica: October 31. People wear giant masks and dance. A cimarrona plays lively music. Artisans make the masks. La Giganta is a giant woman character.

Halloween in California: October 31. Many children wear costumes and say “Trick or treat!” A jack-o’-lantern is a carved pumpkin with a light inside.

Day of the Dead in Mexico: Especially November 1–2. Families honor people who have died. An ofrenda may hold photos, favorite foods, candles, and marigold flowers.

Different traditions have different meanings. Families can celebrate in different ways.
