TRADITION ADVENTURE — GRADE 6 ONLY
Separate activity and separate save key from Grade 8. Suggested 30-minute class.
All student content is in English. No video, no accounts, no camera.

SUGGESTED PACING (30 MINUTES)
0–2: Teacher introduction and student name, section, date.
2–6: Costa Rica photo reading, opinion choice, 4 mini missions.
6–7: Lantern Blaster (60 active seconds).
7–8: 30-second talk + gentle character freeze; brief teacher check-in.
8–12: California photo reading, opinion choice, 4 mini missions.
12–15: Photo Vault memory, FOUR pairs / EIGHT cards.
15–16: 30-second talk + stretch; brief teacher check-in.
16–20: Mexico photo reading, opinion choice, 4 mini missions.
20–21: Mix-Up Machine target boss (60 active seconds).
21–22: 30-second talk + character freeze; brief teacher check-in.
22–28: Pumpkin Lab and Mask Workshop, about 3 minutes each.
28–30: Partner sharing, finish, download record and optional PNG designs.
Teacher-led pacing: actual time varies by reading, memory, and design speed. Use a visible workshop timer. For early finishers: explain one new fact and give a partner a kind design comment. The app does not guarantee exactly 30 minutes or impose a 30-minute lock.

GRADE 6 SUPPORT
Shorter readings, everyday vocabulary, picture clues, three choices per mission, simple hints, no typed reading notes. Students select personal ideas for completion rather than correctness. Reading pages require 45 active seconds plus an idea choice. New questions require 8 active seconds and a response. Three 30-second talk/movement checkpoints follow games. A seated option and solo speech option are always allowed. Students may keep personal family memories private.

GAMES
Lantern Blaster: tap/click bright targets; avoid grey X targets. Left/Right + Space or visible buttons also work.
Photo Vault: four identical photo pairs. Match all four with unlimited attempts. Read each caption.
Mix-Up Machine: pop only the place name shown above the range. Target waves change every five active seconds.
No elimination, no minimum arcade score. These are fictional target games, not representations of cultural rituals. Games pause off-tab and while the guide is open. Unfinished rounds restart on refresh; completed games and lessons remain saved.

DESIGNS AND SUBMISSION
Create a pumpkin and an invented mask inspired by expressive Costa Rican masks. Each requires a name and TWO adjectives. Download PNG designs if requested. Students explain “My mask is…,” “I used…,” or “I like it because….” Finish reveals comprehension score and allows a text adventure record with identity, personal choices, all answers, and game results. Submit in Google Classroom when directed.
Artistic skill, opinions, and game points do not change the English score.
Browser-local autosave, separate key: tradition-junior-grade6-v1. Same browser/device only. Separate profiles on shared devices.
