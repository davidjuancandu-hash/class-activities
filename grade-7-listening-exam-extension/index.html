Your listening passage explained that experiential travel helps visitors connect with local people and their culture. Examples included staying with local hosts, learning a craft, and volunteering at a farm or restaurant.

The passage described Mejdi Tours and a trip in Vietnam: travelers meet a fisherman, learn to repair and throw fishing nets, catch fish, and then cook and eat with the fisherman's family. It also explained how travelers learn about daily life, make lifelong friends, and stay in touch by writing or calling.

You do not need a travel company for every experience. The passage gave examples such as talking with a driver or hotel receptionist, taking a cooking class, or joining a street soccer game. Listening to people's stories and having simple conversations can make a trip more meaningful.
