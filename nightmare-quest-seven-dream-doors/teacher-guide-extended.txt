NIGHTMARE QUEST — THE SEVEN DREAM DOORS
EXTENDED GRADE 7 LESSON: 65–70 MINUTES
Use the same student activity link. The expanded version uses its own save slot and starts a fresh adventure. Older saved results are not deleted.

SUGGESTED 70-MINUTE ROUTE
0–5: PowerPoint introduction (slides 1–7 and 12).
5–40: Seven short video levels, 24 questions, Dream Guide reading and discussion. Spread this work around the workshops and breaks below. Allow roughly five minutes per level, including partner discussion.
15 minutes total: Three five-minute creative workshops, after Levels 2, 4 and 6.
4 minutes total: Dream Spark Hunt, Memory Key Match and Dreamworld Freeze, after those workshops.
2 minutes: Dream Warden memory boss after Level 7.
5 minutes: Dream Architect final invented story.
4 minutes: Review, grade, download and submit.
These are planning targets, not a guaranteed completion time. Give students reading/discussion time and check notebook work. Eight seconds per question is only a minimum.

Lesson 1: Intro, Levels 1–2, Friendly Twist workshop, Spark Hunt.
Lesson 2: Levels 3–4, Repeating Dream Map workshop, Key Match.
Lesson 3: Levels 5–7, Exit Plan workshop, movement break, Warden, final story, review.

WORKSHOP PRODUCTS
1. Friendly Twist: character drawing/labels, three details, setting, problem, two sentences and a helpful twist.
2. Repeating Dream Map: three rooms/paths, one changing detail, two sentences with again and Then I realized, and an exit clue.
3. Dream Exit Plan: character/setting, problem, helpful clue, two sentences, solution and ending emotion.
Each workshop has five minutes of active time with minute-by-minute prompts. Students enter a title and one sentence/detail, then confirm their notebook and speaking work. The teacher checks the notebook.
Timers pause when the game tab is hidden, loses focus, or the guide is open. Keep the game active while students work in notebooks.

GAMES
Dream Spark Hunt: 60 active seconds. Catch stars. No minimum score.
Memory Key Match: find six pairs, unlimited tries.
Dreamworld Freeze: 90 active seconds. Safe seated or standing movements.
Dream Warden: repeat three sequences of 3, 4, and 5 symbols. Unlimited retries. Optional visible clue after each sequence.
Dream Architect: creative story choices followed by a 30-second spoken rehearsal.

SCORING
English grade = correct comprehension answers / 24. No correctness during work. Review/change reached answers before FINISH + GRADE.
Sparks: 50 per completed level, 50 per completed brain break, 100 for defeating the Warden, 100 for completing the final story.
Creative choices, drawing quality and game retries do not lower the English grade.
No student must share personal frightening experiences. Invented stories are welcome. The video game stages are not a medical severity scale.
Videos: seven 30-second clips, 3½ minutes total. English subtitles and reading support remain available.

POWERPOINT
Show slides 1–7 and 12 before independent work. Revisit slides 8–10 during workshops and 11 for the final challenge. Teacher pacing and reminders appear in speaker notes.
