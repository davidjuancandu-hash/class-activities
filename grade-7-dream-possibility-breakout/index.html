🎮 How to play / Teacher guide

Controls: mouse, finger, or arrow keys move the paddle. Space launches the ball. Bricks trigger multiple-choice grammar challenges. The Next Brick Challenge button provides an accessible alternative to aiming, so every student can finish. Answer correctness appears after selection. Progress automatically saves in the same browser.

Scoring: Every question breaks a brick. A correct first answer earns 10 coins and one star. An incorrect answer breaks the brick but earns no coins. The final grade is out of 20 questions. There is no time limit.

Lesson note: These are original practice questions inspired by the Unit 6 overview; they are not copied from unseen textbook exercises.
