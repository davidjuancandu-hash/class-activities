🎮 How to play and classroom notes

Fire with Space or the FIRE button. Move with the arrow keys, mouse, or finger on the game field. Each alien hit brings up one of 20 questions; once answered, the alien disappears. For students who prefer to focus on English, use NEXT ALIEN CHALLENGE to open the next question without aiming. Use STUCK? OPEN ARTICLE at any time to reread while keeping the current game. Answers are saved in the browser. Correctness, final star totals, correct answers, and explanations remain hidden until all 20 invaders are defeated. No visible timer or lost lives. Each new alien question has a quiet 12-second minimum before you can move on.

Questions are original practice based on the Unit 6 introduction (Pages 92–93) and related dream/reality ideas. They do not require unseen textbook pages or research claims.
