1. I want to improve my Portuguese, ___ I'm traveling around Brazil for three months.
Answer: B — so
The language goal is the reason; traveling is the result.

Combine the Ideas
7. Sonya loves to cook. She's taking a food tour of Italy next year. Choose the best combination.
Answer: A — Sonya loves to cook, so she's taking a food tour of Italy next year.
Cooking is the reason and the tour is the result.
Combine the Ideas
8. Ana is interested in Vietnam. She's going to stay on a Vietnamese farm. Complete: 'Ana is going to stay on a Vietnamese farm ___ she's interested in Vietnam.'
Answer: B — because
Because correctly introduces Ana's reason.
Combine the Ideas
9. Chuy wants to get a lot of exercise. He's going on a hiking tour in Peru. Choose the correct opening.
Answer: A — Since Chuy wants to get a lot of exercise, he's going on a hiking tour in Peru.
Since starts a reason clause, followed by a comma.

10. Mona is visiting Beijing for the weekend. She loves walking around the old palaces. Complete: 'Mona is visiting Beijing ___ she loves walking around the old palaces.'
Answer: B — since
Since means because and gives Mona's reason.
Combine the Ideas
11. Which sentence correctly puts the reason FIRST?
Answer: A — Because Ana is interested in Vietnam, she's going to stay on a Vietnamese farm.
A beginning because-clause needs its verb and a comma before the main clause.
Combine the Ideas
12. Which sentence puts a RESULT after 'so'?
Answer: C — Chuy wants exercise, so he's taking a hiking tour.
The so clause tells what Chuy does as a result.
Farm-Stay Editor
13. Read: 'You live with a farming family so you learn a lot about the local culture.' What punctuation is missing?
Answer: A — A comma before 'so'
So joins two independent clauses; put a comma before so.
Farm-Stay Editor
14. Read: 'Because, you work all day with local people you have to use Spanish.' Which correction is best?
Answer: A — Because you work all day with local people, you have to use Spanish.
Remove the comma after Because and place it after the full reason clause.
Farm-Stay Editor
15. Read: 'Farming is physically hard, because you get plenty of exercise every day.' Which edit fits the grammar pattern taught?
Answer: A — Delete the comma before 'because'
A reason clause after the main clause usually does not need that comma.
Farm-Stay Editor
16. In 'You have to use Spanish because you work with local people,' what is the REASON?
Answer: B — You work with local people
The because-clause states the reason.
Farm-Stay Editor
17. Read: 'You work with local people, so you practice Spanish.' What is the RESULT?
Answer: C — You practice Spanish
The so-clause presents the result.

18. Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
Answer: B — Since the farm work is hard, you get exercise.
A comma follows the opening since-clause.
