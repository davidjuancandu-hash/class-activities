🎃 HALLOWEEN MOVIE CLUBGrade 6 · Watch a little. Talk a lot.
Six short clips · Opinions have no wrong answers · Teacher-led or partner play

Welcome to Movie Club!

Watch one tiny clip. Guess the movie, then tell us what YOU think. Haven’t seen it? Say whether you would watch it.

Speak first! Point to an answer, tell a partner your reason, then tap your choice. Your teacher can use one screen for the whole class. Choices on a shared screen represent that discussion, not every student’s vote.

The optional discussion timer is 90 seconds per round. Your teacher can move on earlier or allow more talking. Two 45-second movement breaks follow rounds 2 and 4.

Your choices save on this device under your name. At the end you can download a club record. It is not an automatic submission or an English grade.
