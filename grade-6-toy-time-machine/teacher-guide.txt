TOY TIME MACHINE — GRADE 6 | TEACHER GUIDE
Suggested class length: 25 minutes. Actual pace depends on reading, replays, and discussion.
VIDEO: teacher-supplied TED-Ed animation, What toys have kids played with throughout history?
Section 1: 0:00–1:50; Section 2: 1:50–3:24; Section 3: 3:24–5:02. End credits after 5:02 omitted.

PACING
0–2 min: Introduce the mission and enter student details.
2–8 min: Section 1 + questions 1–5 + one-minute Toy Talk.
8–9 min: Toy Box Catch (45 seconds) and a brief stretch.
9–15 min: Section 2 + questions 6–10 + one-minute Toy Talk.
15–16 min: Toy Shelf Match (45 seconds) and look away from the screen.
16–22 min: Section 3 + questions 11–15 + one-minute Toy Talk.
22–25 min: Choose an imaginary collection, explain a reason, review, grade, download.

HOW IT WORKS
Each video must be viewed before its questions unlock. First viewing limits forward seeking; completed clips allow free seeking. Playback pauses when the tab is hidden.
Questions have an eight-second minimum on first arrival. Previously reached questions can be reviewed without repeating the wait. Eight seconds is a minimum, not a response deadline.
Correctness is hidden until FINISH + GRADE. Students can change answers before grading.
The English score is correct answers out of 15. Each answered mission gives 10 play tokens; each completed break gives 50. Tokens do not depend on correctness and do not affect the grade.
No minimum game score is required. Both games last 45 active seconds. Speaking checkpoints last 60 active seconds, split into 30 seconds per partner. Timers pause when the tab is hidden or the Toy Guide is open.
Personal collection preferences are completion tasks, not marked right or wrong.
Autosave uses this browser/device. Download does not automatically submit to Google Classroom.
Use individual devices for individual records. On a projector, one shared record represents the class discussion.
Questions include video observations, vocabulary, and applying the ideas. The embedded support notes help students without textbooks.
