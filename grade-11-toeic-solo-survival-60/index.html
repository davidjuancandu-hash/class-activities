📘 Mission 3 — TOEIC Survival Review

10 minutes. Study these patterns before the final exam.

Workplace vocabulary: invoice = bill requesting payment; refund = money returned; deadline = final completion date; shipment = goods being sent; supervisor = person managing staff; postpone = move to a later time.

Grammar traps: will + base verb (will arrive); have/has + past participle (has arrived); by Friday (no later than Friday); due to + noun (due to rain); look forward to + -ing (look forward to meeting).

Test strategy: Eliminate answers with the wrong word form. Listen for dates, locations, purposes, and changes. Never choose an answer simply because it repeats a word from the question.
