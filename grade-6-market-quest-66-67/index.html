🏮 The textbook photo

The market is Khan el-Khalili in Cairo, Egypt. People shop for local crafts and souvenirs. The photo shows colorful lamps and hanging lights. A souvenir helps you remember a place or a trip.

🪙 Money words + practice wallets

Spend: use money to buy something. Save: keep money for later. Budget: plan how to spend and save. Compare: look at two options before choosing.

In our story, you need a pencil for class but only want a glitter toy. Needs depend on the situation. All prices are fictional game tokens.

  • 12 − 5 = 7 tokens left.
  • A goal of 20 with 8 saved means 12 more are needed.
  • The same notebook costs 8 at A and 10 at B: A costs less.
  • 15 − 6 − 2 = 7 saved after buying a notebook and pencil.
📱 Think before you click

Advertisements promote products. A paid promotion means a company paid someone to show a product; it does not prove it is the best. Pause before buying, compare prices, consider your budget and follow adult permission rules. Keep passwords private. Tell a trusted adult about suspicious messages.

🎯 Actions happening now

Present continuous = am / is / are + verb-ing.

  • I am saving.
  • She is buying a souvenir.
  • They are shopping.
  • He is not spending money.
  • Are they comparing prices?

Give a clear tip: “Compare prices and follow your shopping plan.”
