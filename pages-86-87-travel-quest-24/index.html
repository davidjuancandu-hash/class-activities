📖 FIRST STOP • TRAVEL GUIDE

Read before your adventure

Take time to understand these examples. You can reopen this guide while answering any question.

Writing Skill: Write a concluding sentence. The last sentence in a paragraph is usually the concluding sentence. A concluding sentence tells the reader you have finished explaining the main idea. You can restate the main idea using different words, give an opinion, make a prediction, or make a suggestion.

Restatement: In summary, travel has physical, emotional, and mental benefits.

Opinion: I believe that experiential travel is better for travelers and locals.

Prediction: You will understand Vietnam better if you take the Mekong River tour.

Suggestion: If you want a relaxing vacation, I suggest you take a hot springs tour of Japan.

Volunteer work trip topic sentence: A volunteer work trip is a great way to travel because you get job experience, you learn about the local culture, and you help people.

Grammar: We use because or since to introduce a reason. Because/since + subject + verb forms an adverb clause of reason. An adverb clause is a dependent clause. It can come before or after the main clause. When it comes first, use a comma.

Experiential travelers learn more about a place because they stay with local people.

Since they stay with local people, experiential travelers learn more about a place.

We use so to introduce a result. So connects two independent clauses.

Travelers stay with local people, so they learn more about a place.
