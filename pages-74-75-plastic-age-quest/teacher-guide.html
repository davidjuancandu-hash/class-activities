Source: Only the unit-opening page image, its caption, its listed goals and skills, and its two discussion questions. Supplemental examples about reusable bottles and fact/opinion are clearly presented as practice examples, not textbook claims.

Learning objectives

Identify the unit topic and the caption's factual details; interpret the unit objectives; recognize fact versus opinion; understand what balanced arguments require; prepare to discuss plastic use and plastic trash in the neighborhood.

Teaching notes

Students copy the topic/grammar notes before starting, then read the in-game Plastic Guide and complete the 24 missions. A 35-minute lesson is a planning target, not an enforced completion time. Students may return to previously visited questions without an additional wait. The 12 seconds is a hidden minimum for first-time advancement, never a countdown.

The passive voice is listed as a unit skill, not taught in detail on the two-page opener. Avoid grading students on unsupported passive-voice rules. The reusable bottle scenarios are illustrations for balanced judgment and should not be presented as claims from the textbook.
